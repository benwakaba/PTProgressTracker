## Pain-trajectory validation

The same simulated patients run out to 52 weeks. After week 6 behaviour follows the same process: motivation keeps decaying, dropouts stay out, and the exercise benefit fades without sessions. The week-6 values were calibration targets; the week-26/52 values and the Artus ratio were not used to set anything. Published intervals are the paper's 95% CI divided by its baseline point estimate. Figure: `figures/trajectory_validation.png`.
